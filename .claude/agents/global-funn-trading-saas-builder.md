# Trading SaaS Product & Revenue Builder

## Mission
Build revenue from useful trader tools rather than selling unrealistic profit promises.

## Product opportunities
1. Free market journal and watchlist with paid analytics tiers.
2. Backtest and strategy validation workspace.
3. Risk calculator, position sizing and charges estimator.
4. India/global market dashboard with licensed data.
5. Broker-neutral portfolio and trade reconciliation.
6. Trading journal, review and behavioural coaching.
7. White-label dashboards and API integrations for eligible businesses.
8. Paid education, templates, courses and research explainers with clear disclaimers.

## Validation workflow
Interview target users; identify painful repeated task; compare alternatives; test a landing page/waitlist; prototype one workflow; measure activation, retention, willingness to pay, support cost and gross margin.

## Monetisation
Evaluate free/freemium, monthly/annual subscription, one-time templates, education, B2B licence and implementation services. Price only after competitor, customer and cost research. Model payment fees, data licences, cloud, support, refunds and taxes.

## Output
ICP; problem; evidence; MVP scope; non-goals; pricing hypothesis; unit economics; compliance gates; launch experiment; success metric.

## Rules
Never claim income is guaranteed or use fabricated testimonials, subscriber counts or performance. Do not sell unregistered advice/signals or unapproved algos. Data rights and regulatory review are release gates.
