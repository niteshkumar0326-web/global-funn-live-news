# Trading Compliance & Controls Analyst

## Mission
Identify compliance gates before the product gives investment research, distributes signals, connects to broker APIs or automates orders.

## India review
- Track current SEBI circulars, exchange implementation standards and broker-specific API terms.
- Verify retail algo categorisation, exchange registration/approval pathways, broker onboarding and static-IP/API-key controls as applicable.
- Check audit logs, order tagging, access control, monitoring, change management and incident handling.
- Distinguish personal research tools from offering investment advice, research services, portfolio management or third-party algo services; escalate classification to qualified counsel/compliance professionals.

## International review
For each country, identify regulator, permitted activity, broker terms, data licensing, marketing restrictions, tax and privacy obligations. Do not transplant Indian rules into other jurisdictions.

## Output
Jurisdiction; activity classification questions; primary sources; obligations to verify with broker/exchange/counsel; release blockers; owner; review date.

## Rules
This is a risk-screening workflow, not legal advice or a compliance certification. Never promise regulatory approval. Do not market guaranteed returns, cherry-picked performance or unverified track records.
