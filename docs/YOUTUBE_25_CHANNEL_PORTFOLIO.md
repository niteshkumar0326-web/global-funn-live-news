# GLOBAL FUNN — 25-Channel Portfolio Strategy

## Operating principle
These are 25 candidate channel brands, not a recommendation to launch all at once. Validate 3 pilots first. Keep each channel distinct in audience, original editorial value, format and brand. Do not upload the same video across many channels with superficial changes.

## Pilot scorecard
Score each candidate 1–5 for: audience demand, competition gap, creator expertise/access, repeatability, monetisation options, production cost, rights/safety risk. Weight demand, repeatability and ability to sell a product/service most heavily. Do not treat trend volume as guaranteed views.

## SEO and trend workflow
For each proposed video:
1. Find topic clusters in Google Trends (India, YouTube Search), YouTube search suggestions, current news and audience comments.
2. Check trend direction over 7, 30 and 90 days; separate seasonal spikes from evergreen demand.
3. Review 5–10 competing videos: title intent, freshness, format, unanswered questions and visible audience feedback.
4. Build one primary query, 3–5 supporting phrases, a clear viewer promise and a unique angle.
5. Draft title variants, opening hook, chapters, description, source links and thumbnail concept.
6. Never keyword-stuff or promise a result the video does not deliver.
7. Review YouTube Analytics after 48 hours, 7 days and 28 days: impressions, CTR, retention, returning viewers, traffic source, subscribers per 1,000 views and revenue where eligible.

## Scale gates
- Stage 1: 3 pilots; publish consistently for 8–12 weeks.
- Stage 2: retain only channels with a repeatable audience signal and manageable production.
- Stage 3: launch 2–3 additional channels using proven templates and distinct editorial teams.
- Stage 4: expand towards 25 only after staffing, rights management, quality checks and positive unit economics exist.

## Content integrity
YouTube may reject monetisation for repetitive, mass-produced or reused content without meaningful original value. Every channel needs distinct original research, commentary, storytelling, teaching or reporting. Follow the current YouTube monetisation policies before scaling.
