## Recording direction
- Voice: clear, conversational Hindi; informed explainer, not sensational anchor delivery.
- Pace: approximately 135–150 Hindi words per minute; pause after each key number.
- Record each sentence as a separate take to simplify retakes.
- Pronounce AI-heavy as “ए-आई से जुड़ी बड़ी कंपनियां”; $12.21bn as “12.21 अरब डॉलर”.
- Avoid dramatic crash, guaranteed-profit, or “smart money knows” language.
- Use clean close-mic audio and leave 0.3–0.5 seconds between sentences.
