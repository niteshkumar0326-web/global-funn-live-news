## 1. Editing route

Use Microsoft Clipchamp in a desktop browser or Windows app. This is a manual, low-cost route and does not depend on Descript AI credits or fal video generation. Do not purchase a plan, enable paid stock, or spend generation credits without explicit owner approval.

- Long-form canvas: 16:9, 1920 × 1080, 30 fps.
- Shorts canvas: 9:16, 1080 × 1920, 30 fps.
- Export: 1080p review copy first. Use a private/unlisted review stage; do not publish automatically.
- Keep all original phone recordings untouched in a separate folder.

## 3. Long-form assembly order

1. Create a new 16:9 project and import the seven original narration files.
2. Place narration in approved script order on the primary audio track. Listen from start to finish before adding visuals.
3. Remove only obvious dead air, handling noise and duplicated takes. Do not cut breaths so tightly that speech sounds unnatural.
4. Add visuals above the narration and align each cut to the actual spoken sentence. The time ranges in the cue sheet are provisional only.
5. Add a short opening title, clear illustrative-reenactment label where applicable, source/reporting slide, and GLOBAL FUNN end card.
6. Add captions using Clipchamp's available caption workflow or manually authored text. Correct every Hindi word, acronym, number and URL by listening to the actual narration.
7. Add only licensed/original background music. Keep it low enough that every instruction remains intelligible; no music is required if no verified track is available.
8. Watch the complete video once with sound and once muted. Check phone-sized readability.
9. Export a 1080p private review copy. Do not use provisional chapter timestamps as final chapters until the real edit is measured.

## 4. Visual sequence and editorial locks

| Script section | Visual direction | Required on-screen emphasis |

| Family routine | Family discussion and private/locked safe-word symbol | रुकें → पुष्टि करें → फिर कार्रवाई करें |
| Reporting | Clear reporting card: bank, 1930, cybercrime.gov.in | बैंक को बताएं • 1930 • cybercrime.gov.in |
| Closing | GLOBAL FUNN end card | तकनीक को समझिए, सुरक्षित रहिए |

Never show a real person's likeness, actual family safe word, real bank account or payment screen, working QR code, fake police uniform, government seal, fabricated victim story, or private message. Label reenactments as illustrative. Do not teach blinking, lip-sync, skin texture, lighting or voice glitches as dependable deepfake detectors.

## 5. Shorts workflow

Create two separate 9:16 projects; do not simply crop the long-form master without checking composition.

**Short 1 — family voice asking for money**
- Hook: परिवार की आवाज़ में पैसे मांगे तो?
- Sequence: fictional incoming call → end call → separate callback to saved contact → private safe-word symbol → OTP/UPI PIN warning → share prompt.
- Keep the safe word itself off-screen.

**Short 2 — after a financial cyber scam**
- Hook: Deepfake scam हो गया?
- Sequence: tell bank immediately → 1930 → cybercrime.gov.in → transaction ID / number / message / timestamp / screenshot evidence → no-recovery-guarantee reminder.
- Verify the helpline and URL on edit day before export.

## 6. Caption and audio QA

- Use Devanagari for Hindi speech and retain English technical terms where the approved script uses them.
- Keep captions to one or two short lines and break at natural pauses.
- Use high contrast and safe margins; preview on a small phone screen.
- Manually verify CERT-In, OTP, UPI PIN, 1930 and cybercrime.gov.in.
- Confirm voice is clear, not clipped, and consistent in volume.
- Preserve raw audio; never overwrite originals with denoised or processed versions.
- Check that captions match the actual final narration, not a stale script copy.
