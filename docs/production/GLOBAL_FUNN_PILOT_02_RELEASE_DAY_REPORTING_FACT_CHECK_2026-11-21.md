## Confirmed official reporting wording
- The National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal is the Government of India's portal for reporting cybercrime incidents: https://cybercrime.gov.in
- The portal currently displays: for cyber financial fraud, immediate reporting is available through 1930 (24×7).
- Ministry of Home Affairs / PIB release dated 28 July 2026 describes the Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud Reporting and Management System (CFCFRMS) as intended for immediate reporting of financial frauds and says the toll-free helpline 1930 is operationalized to assist with lodging online cyber complaints.
- The portal's reporting information and MHA/PIB material support the video's distinction: 1930 is specifically emphasized for financial cyber fraud; do not describe it as the exclusive reporting route for every type of deepfake harm.
