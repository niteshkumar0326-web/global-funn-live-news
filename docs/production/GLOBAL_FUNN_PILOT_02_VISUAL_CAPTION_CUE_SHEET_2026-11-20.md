## Caption construction rules
- Use Hindi Devanagari for Hindi narration; retain standard English technical terms where the script uses them.
- Keep captions to one or two short lines at a time. Break at natural spoken pauses, not arbitrary character counts.
- Use high contrast, a plain background or subtle shadow, and safe margins so text is not covered by YouTube controls.
- Emphasize only the actionable phrases: “कॉल काटिए”, “सेव नंबर”, “safe word”, “OTP”, “UPI PIN”, “1930”.
- Never emphasize a number or URL until it has been checked against the final narration and official source.
- Do not auto-correct names, acronyms, helpline numbers or URLs without human review.
- Check captions on a small phone screen at normal playback speed.

## Shorts visual recipes
### Short 1 — family voice asking for money
1. Open on a generic incoming video-call visual; hook text: “परिवार की आवाज़ में पैसे मांगे तो?”
2. Cut to the call ending; show a separate callback to a saved contact.
3. Show a private safe-word symbol, not the actual word.
4. Flash “OTP / UPI PIN किसी को न बताएं”.
5. Close on “पहले पुष्टि करें — फिर फैसला लें” and GLOBAL FUNN.

### Short 2 — after a deepfake-related financial scam
1. Open with a calm urgent title: “Deepfake scam हो गया?”
2. Show “बैंक को तुरंत बताएं”.
3. Display “1930” large and legible.
4. Display “cybercrime.gov.in” exactly; verify spelling and current availability before export.
5. Show evidence items: transaction ID, phone number, message, timestamp, screenshots.
6. Close with “जल्दी रिपोर्ट करें — पैसे वापसी की गारंटी नहीं” and GLOBAL FUNN.

## Asset sourcing and rights
- Prefer original vector shapes, self-created phone mockups and licensed stock.
- Do not use real victim photos, private chat screenshots, bank logos/screens, government seals, police uniforms or celebrity/public-figure likenesses as decorative shorthand.
- Any reenactment must be clearly illustrative.
- Keep a source/permission note for every third-party image, clip, font and music track.
- If no verified asset is available, use a simple text card or original graphic instead of a misleading image.
