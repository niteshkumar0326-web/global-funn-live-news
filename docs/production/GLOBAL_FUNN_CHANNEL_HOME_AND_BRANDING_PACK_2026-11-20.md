## 1. Positioning
**Promise:** Clear Hindi explainers on the developments shaping India and the world — with practical context, verified sources and no unnecessary hype.

**Primary audience:** Hindi-speaking viewers who want to understand current affairs, technology/AI, cyber safety and money/market concepts in plain language.

**Editorial pillars**
1. India & world: what happened, why it matters, what may happen next.
2. Technology & AI: useful developments, real-world applications, risks and limitations.
3. Cyber safety: scams, deepfakes, privacy and practical protection.
4. Money & markets education: explainers, not personalized investment calls or guaranteed-return claims.

Keep unrelated entertainment, children's content, devotional fiction and shopping experiments out of this flagship channel's future release queue. Do not mass-delete or unlist existing videos; review them individually before any channel cleanup.

## 2. Channel About — ready-to-paste draft
GLOBAL FUNN brings you clear, practical Hindi explainers on India, world affairs, technology, artificial intelligence, cyber safety and money & markets.

We focus on three questions: What happened? Why does it matter? What should an ordinary viewer understand or do next?

Our videos aim to separate verified facts from opinion, explain complex topics in simple language, and point viewers to credible sources. We do not promise investment returns, offer personalized financial advice, or encourage panic and misinformation.

- **AI & Technology — Practical Guides:** Understand AI tools, technology trends, real applications, limitations and responsible use.
- **Cyber Safety — Protect Yourself:** Practical guidance on scams, deepfakes, online fraud, privacy and safer digital habits.
- **Money & Markets — Learn the Basics:** Educational explainers on the economy, business and markets. Not personalized investment advice.
- **GLOBAL FUNN Shorts:** Short, focused explainers and practical takeaways from the GLOBAL FUNN team.

## 6. Visual consistency rules
- Use one consistent logo/avatar and a legible banner; do not introduce multiple competing identities.
- Banner message: **India • World • AI & Tech • Cyber Safety • Money Explained**
- Keep thumbnail text to one short Hindi promise; use consistent typography and restrained colors.
- Show source/date labels on time-sensitive explainers.
- Never use fabricated charts, misleading breaking-news styling, or visuals that imply an event occurred when it did not.
