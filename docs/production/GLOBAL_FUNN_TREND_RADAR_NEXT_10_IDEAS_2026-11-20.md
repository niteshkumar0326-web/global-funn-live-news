## Production rule

- Every shortlisted topic must pass: source quality, audience fit, originality, production cost, copyright safety and fact-check gates.
- Research title keywords separately from trend-chart position. A trending video is not proof of high search volume.
- Use official sources first for product launches, safety advice, regulations and financial data; use reputable reporting for context.
- Do not copy competitor scripts, footage, thumbnails or distinctive branding.
- For each approved topic create: one long-form script, two Shorts, metadata, thumbnail brief, source ledger and release checklist.
- Human approval required before publication.
