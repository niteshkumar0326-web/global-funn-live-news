# GLOBAL FUNN Pilot 02 — Two Shorts Edit Blueprint
Date: 2026-11-21
Status: EDITING PLAN ONLY — audio, edit timelines and exports are not yet verified.

## Shared export and editorial settings
- Canvas: vertical 9:16, 1080 × 1920, 30 fps.
- Use the approved standalone Short 1 and Short 2 scripts from the Pilot 02 production pack / free TTS chunking sheet. Do not paraphrase narration without owner review.
- Narration is the master track. Timing below is a shot order, not fixed timestamps; cut visuals and captions to the actual spoken audio.
- Keep captions in the central safe area, away from the top and bottom UI overlays. Use short Hindi phrases, high contrast and no more than two lines at once.
- Use original graphics or rights-cleared media only. No real-person likenesses, private chats, real bank screens, government seals, working QR codes or actual family safe words.
- Music is optional. If used, it must be original or properly licensed, low under narration, and documented.
- Export review copies only. No public upload or scheduling without explicit owner approval.

## Short 1 — Family voice asking for money
**Core viewer action:** end the call and independently call the person's previously saved number.

### Shot order
1. Hook: generic fictional incoming video-call silhouette.
   - On-screen text: “परिवार की आवाज़ में पैसे मांगे तो?”
   - Clearly illustrative; do not imitate a real person.
2. Action: call ends; a separate callback is initiated from a saved-contact concept screen.
   - On-screen text: “कॉल काटें — सेव नंबर पर खुद कॉल करें”
3. Family protection: family icon with a locked/private-word symbol.
   - On-screen text: “घर में private safe word तय करें”
   - Never show or speak an actual safe word.
4. Credential safety: shield graphic with OTP / UPI PIN labels.
   - On-screen text: “OTP / UPI PIN कभी न बताएं”
5. Close: GLOBAL FUNN end card.
   - On-screen text: “पहले पुष्टि करें — फिर फैसला लें”

### Audio / caption checkpoints
- Preserve the exact approved Short 1 narration.
- Emphasize the spoken phrases about not transferring immediately, calling the saved number, safe word, and OTP / UPI PIN.
- Captions must not introduce a new instruction absent from the narration.
- Confirm the last call-to-action remains readable before the video ends.

## Short 2 — What to do after financial cyber fraud
**Core viewer action:** contact the bank promptly, call 1930 for financial cyber fraud, and report at the official portal.

### Shot order
1. Calm alert title card: “Financial cyber fraud?”
2. Bank action card: “तुरंत बैंक को सूचित करें”
3. Helpline card: “1930” large and legible, with “वित्तीय साइबर धोखाधड़ी” nearby.
4. Portal card: “cybercrime.gov.in” spelled exactly.
5. Evidence card: transaction ID, phone number, messages, time and screenshots.
6. Closing card: “जल्दी रिपोर्ट करें — पैसे वापसी की गारंटी नहीं” plus GLOBAL FUNN.

### Accuracy / safety checkpoints
- Keep 1930 explicitly scoped to financial cyber fraud; do not present it as the exclusive route for every deepfake-related harm.
- Recheck the official portal URL and current helpline wording on upload day.
- Do not promise refunds, recovery, guaranteed fund blocking or a response time.
- Do not show a real victim's personal details or an actual transaction screenshot.
- Preserve the approved narration's caution that money recovery is not guaranteed.
