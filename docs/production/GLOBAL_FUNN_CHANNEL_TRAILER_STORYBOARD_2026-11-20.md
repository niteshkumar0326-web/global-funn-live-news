## Recording direction
- One natural Hindi voice; warm, confident, conversational, not sensational.
- Aim for 105–120 words per minute; leave small pauses between editorial pillars.
- Record in a quiet room, phone microphone 15–20 cm away, slightly off-axis to reduce plosives.
- Capture one complete take plus a second take of the opening and closing lines.
- Record 5 seconds of room tone. Do not add loud background music under speech.

## Edit and accessibility
- Master: 1920×1080, 16:9, 30 fps; audio 48 kHz where supported.
- Keep important text inside the central safe area; test on a phone screen.
- Burned-in Hindi captions must be checked word-by-word against the final audio.
- Use licensed/original music and visuals only; retain source/asset records.
- Keep transitions simple; avoid flashing effects and clutter.
- Export review copy first. Check opening clarity, audio peaks/clipping, caption spelling, final CTA and mobile legibility before upload.
