# GLOBAL FUNN — Pilot 02 Hindi Narration Direction Sheet
**Date:** 20 November 2026  
**Purpose:** Help the narrator deliver the approved Hindi script clearly and naturally. This sheet does not replace or alter the fact-checked script.

## Voice and delivery
- Language: conversational Hindi with familiar English technology terms, as written in the approved script.
- Tone: calm, alert and practical. Do not sound frightening, theatrical or sales-heavy.
- Pace: approximately 95–115 Hindi words per minute for explanatory sections. Slow down for instructions, numbers and reporting details.
- Energy: slightly stronger on the opening question and the three-part safety rule; steady and reassuring in the reporting section.
- Pauses: leave a short pause after each key instruction and a longer pause before the final call to action.
- Pronunciation: say “Deepfake” consistently; pronounce CERT-In as “सर्ट-इन”; say “OTP” letter by letter; say “UPI PIN” clearly; read 1930 consistently as either “उन्नीस सौ तीस” or “एक-नौ-तीन-शून्य”; pronounce cybercrime.gov.in slowly and show the URL on screen.
- Do not add new examples, statistics, legal interpretations or guarantees while recording.

## Recording checks
1. Record one complete read-through without stopping to identify difficult phrases.
2. Listen once and mark only genuine errors: wrong number, missing word, unclear pronunciation, clipping or background noise.
3. Re-record the affected section rather than repeatedly restarting the entire video.
4. Keep the final take and the original take until the edit is approved.
5. Review every spoken number, helpline and web address against the production pack before import.

## Shorts delivery
- Short 1: quick hook, then calm instructions. Pause between callback, safe word and OTP/UPI PIN.
- Short 2: urgent but not panicked. Read “1930” and “cybercrime.gov.in” distinctly; end without implying guaranteed recovery.
- Keep each Short separate. Do not speed up narration unnaturally to force it into the time limit; edit pauses first.

## Editorial guardrails
- Never claim that blinking, lip-sync, skin texture or voice glitches reliably identify a deepfake.
- The central message remains: **रुकें → अलग तरीके से पुष्टि करें → फिर कार्रवाई करें**.
- Do not reveal an actual family safe word in narration, captions, comments or visuals.
- Use the current source ledger and recording-day refresh in the main production pack for final fact-checking.
