## 1. CERT-In NCSAM 2026 AI booklet

Official PDF: https://www.csk.gov.in/documents/CERT-In-AI-Booklet-NCSAM-2026.pdf

Page 5, “Voice & Video Deepfakes (Vishing)” supports the core viewer routine:
- Hang up and call back using the known, saved number.
- Set a family/workplace secret safe word.
- Never authorize payment based on a call or video alone; confirm through a separate channel.
- Pause before reacting.

The booklet explains that attackers can clone voices or create convincing fake videos and may pressure people to share sensitive information, send money or act quickly.

## 2. Ministry of Home Affairs / PIB reporting route

Official PIB answer, Lok Sabha, submitted 5 August 2026:  
https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2295500&lang=1&reg=48

The release states that:
- The National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal at https://cybercrime.gov.in accepts reports of deepfakes, financial fraud and content misuse.
- Helpline 1930 is functional.
- Police investigate cybercrimes; the relevant law-enforcement route depends on the incident.

On-screen guidance should distinguish immediate financial-fraud response (contact the bank promptly and call 1930) from other deepfake harms, which can also be reported through the portal and local police as appropriate. Do not imply that 1930 is the only route for every harm or promise recovery of funds.

## 3. MeitY / PIB legal-framework context

Official PIB release, 30 July 2026:  
https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2291671&lang=1&reg=48

The government summary describes the IT Rules amendments of 10 February 2026 addressing synthetically generated information, including labelling/traceable metadata for permissible AI-generated content and platform obligations. Present this only as government-reported context. Do not offer legal interpretation, predict penalties or imply every deepfake is automatically unlawful.

## 4. Editorial accuracy locks

- Do not teach blinking, lip-sync, skin texture, lighting or voice oddities as dependable deepfake detectors. At most, describe them as possible clues; they are not a reliable test.
- Independent verification through a known, separately initiated channel remains the primary viewer action.
- Never show or reveal an actual family safe word.
- Use fictional reenactments clearly labelled as illustrative; no real person's likeness, fake police uniform, fabricated victim story or fake government/bank screen.
- Recheck official links and 1930 immediately before recording and before publication.
- Preserve the existing human fact-check, copyright, private/unlisted playback and owner-approval release gates.
