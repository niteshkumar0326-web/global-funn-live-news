## 2. Main strategic diagnosis
The channel is currently trying to serve multiple audiences with very different viewing intents. A person who subscribes for market news may not want children's songs, devotional animation, shopping guides or serial episodes in the same feed. This makes the channel promise harder to understand and makes audience-response comparisons less useful.

The answer is not to delete existing videos or stop creative exploration. The answer is to establish a clear flagship promise on GLOBAL FUNN and move future unrelated verticals to separate channel brands only when there is a repeatable production capacity.

## 3. Recommended flagship positioning
**GLOBAL FUNN: Hindi explainers on India, technology, money and digital life.**

Primary pillars:
1. **India & world explainers:** verified national developments, policy changes and practical public-interest stories.
2. **Technology & AI:** useful AI tools, consumer technology and practical demonstrations.
3. **Digital safety:** scams, deepfakes, privacy and cyber-safety guidance.
4. **Money & markets education:** explainers, not stock tips or guaranteed-return claims.

Keep each video anchored to one audience question. Use sources and date labels on time-sensitive reporting. Separate education from investment advice; avoid sensational or unsupported certainty.

## 4. What should not be mixed into the flagship feed
Future children's songs, devotional stories, fictional serials, movie uploads, general shopping and unrelated course/library promotion should be held for separate channel brands or a clearly defined separate publishing plan. Do not mass-create or launch additional channels until each has a distinct audience promise, rights-cleared content supply, owner-approved branding and a sustainable schedule.

Existing uploads should not be mass-deleted or made private as part of this plan. Any changes to existing videos require an individual review and owner approval.

## 5. 30-day practical reset
### Before the next upload
- Confirm channel About text, banner and profile promise match the flagship positioning.
- Build four playlists matching the primary pillars.
- Use consistent title language: viewer problem + clear outcome; avoid keyword stuffing and exaggerated claims.
- Use one recognizable thumbnail system while keeping each thumbnail truthful to the story.
- Add a source/date note in descriptions for current affairs and financial explainers.

### First four-week content test
- Publish one substantial, fact-checked Hindi explainer per week.
- Create up to two Shorts from each long video, each with a self-contained point and no misleading teaser.
- Rotate the four pillars, but do not publish unrelated entertainment/children's/devotional content on this flagship during the test.
- Review 24-hour and 7-day analytics before deciding the next topic.

### Decision rules
- Compare like with like: Shorts against Shorts, long-form against long-form, and similar topic/length where possible.
- Use impressions and CTR together; use retention and average view duration to diagnose the video after the click.
- Do not declare a winning pillar from one upload or a tiny sample.
- If the sample is too small or analytics are unavailable, label the result **inconclusive**.
- Do not buy views, subscribers, comments or engagement.
