## Purpose
Use this scorecard for every long-form video and its related Shorts. Record actual YouTube Studio values; never fill unknown metrics with estimates. Compare videos with similar formats and traffic sources rather than treating one universal benchmark as a guarantee.

- [ ] Copyright/music/visual rights checked.
- [ ] Synthetic or altered content disclosure assessed in YouTube Studio.

## E. Decision rules for the next upload
1. **Weak click-through relative to the channel's own comparable videos:** test a clearer title/thumbnail promise; do not change the content topic based on one small sample.
2. **Strong clicks but early audience drop:** make the opening faster and deliver the promised answer sooner.
3. **Good retention but low impressions:** check topic demand, packaging and distribution; avoid claiming the algorithm has penalized the channel.
4. **Shorts get views but few subscribers or long-form visits:** strengthen the relevant follow-up invitation and make the relationship between Short and long video explicit.
5. **Comments show confusion or factual correction:** correct the description or video where needed, pin a clarification, and update the source ledger.
6. **A topic outperforms comparable uploads on multiple meaningful metrics:** create one closely related follow-up; do not immediately multiply channels or dilute production quality.

These are diagnostic prompts, not fixed numerical thresholds. Establish channel-specific baselines after enough comparable uploads.

## H. Guardrails
- Do not buy views, subscribers, comments or watch time.
- Do not promise viral reach, guaranteed earnings or monetization.
- Do not judge a video only by the first few hours.
- Do not change multiple variables at once and then claim to know which change worked.
- Do not publish unverified current-affairs numbers or outdated reporting instructions.
- Keep a dated record of Studio metrics and the observation window.
