# GLOBAL FUNN Pilot 02 — Zero-Cost Phone TTS Quickstart
Date: 2026-11-21
Status: OPERATOR HANDOFF — not a record of completed conversions.

## Goal
Create five final MP3s on an Android phone using only a genuinely free standard Hindi voice. The budget ceiling is ₹0. Do not pay, subscribe, buy credits or fund a wallet.

## Before opening a TTS site
1. Open the [Free TTS Chunking Sheet](GLOBAL_FUNN_PILOT_02_FREE_TTS_CHUNKING_SHEET_2026-11-21.md).
2. Keep it available so the exact approved text can be copied without retyping.
3. Decide whether the provider's privacy/processing terms are acceptable. ClipUtils may process speech on its server when used on Android; do not assume on-device conversion.
4. If the page asks for payment, credits, sign-up that you do not want, or any wallet funding, stop and use no alternative that requires spend.

## Conversion order
Use the same standard Hindi male voice and Normal speed throughout. Do not change voice or speed between chunks.

1. Main A — convert PART1, PART2, PART3 in order.
2. Main B — convert PART1, PART2 in order.
3. Main C — convert PART1, PART2 in order.
4. Short 1 — convert its single chunk.
5. Short 2 — convert its single chunk.

TTS Maker no-signup workflow uses the exact nine subchunks in the chunking sheet (7 main-video chunks + 2 Shorts). Do not exceed the live page's displayed limit. If a chunk is rejected, do not silently trim or paraphrase it; pause and report the exact error.

## Download and name
Use the filenames in the Audio Intake & Assembly Tracker. Download each part separately; do not overwrite an earlier part. Confirm each download is an MP3 and that the file opens/plays.

## Join the main narration parts
Only after all nine source MP3s are downloaded:
- Main A = PART1 + PART2 + PART3.
- Main B = PART1 + PART2.
- Main C = PART1 + PART2.
Keep Short 1 and Short 2 as their own files.

Use a free editor already installed on the phone only if it can join audio without a watermark, trial restriction, paid export or forced upload. If no suitable free editor is already available, do not install a paid app or purchase anything; attach the individual MP3 parts in this chat and pause for an alternate zero-cost assembly path.

## Listen before handoff
Play all five final MP3s from beginning to end. Check clipped starts/ends, missing or repeated sentences, pronunciation, pauses, volume consistency and correct order. Do not use a file that has not been listened to.

## Stop conditions
- Any payment, credit, subscription or wallet request.
- Provider privacy terms are unclear or unacceptable.
- A chunk is truncated, changed, rejected or spoken incorrectly.
- Download is not a playable MP3.
- No safe free joiner is available on the phone.

In every stop case, preserve the approved text and do not spend money.
