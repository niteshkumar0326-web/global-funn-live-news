## 4. Execution checklist

### Before recording
- [ ] Confirm Hindi voice, presenter/voiceover approach and channel identity.
- [ ] Re-open every cited primary source and note access date.
- [ ] Check factual claims, dates, figures, product names and official links.
- [ ] Replace illustrative mockups with licensed or original visuals.
- [ ] Confirm no personal, customer or financial credentials appear in screen recordings.

### Before editing approval
- [ ] Record clean voiceover and keep raw files.
- [ ] Edit long-form master and two vertical Shorts.
- [ ] Sync Hindi captions; review names, numbers and URLs manually.
- [ ] Add source/date cards and disclaimers where needed.
- [ ] Preview on a phone with sound and without sound.

### Before publishing
- [ ] Human fact-check sign-off.
- [ ] Copyright and music-license check.
- [ ] Thumbnail, title, description, tags and pinned comment checked.
- [ ] Private/unlisted playback check.
- [ ] Explicit owner approval for public release and scheduling.

Do not force a fixed upload count before production capacity and quality are demonstrated. Start with one strong long-form video and two Shorts per cycle, then scale based on actual completion and audience data.

## 6. Analytics review template

Capture after 24 hours, 7 days and 28 days:
- Impressions and click-through rate
- Views and unique viewers
- Average view duration and average percentage viewed
- First 30-second retention and key audience-retention dips
- Traffic sources and search terms where available
- Subscribers gained per video
- Shorts viewed vs swiped away and average percentage viewed
- Comments/questions that suggest follow-up topics

Do not label a video viral or successful from views alone. Compare against the channel's own baseline and the age/format of each upload.
