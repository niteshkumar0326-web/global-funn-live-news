# GLOBAL FUNN LIVE NEWS — Editorial and Safety Policy

## Core standard
**No proof, no verified-news publication.** Routine publication may run automatically only when every configured evidence, attribution, rights, quality and platform-policy gate passes. The system must fail closed when evidence or checks are missing.

## Evidence and attribution
- Never invent stories, quotes, sources, documents, URLs, dates, images or eyewitness accounts.
- A source URL is a pointer to inspect, not proof by itself.
- Verify the original source, author/publisher, publication date, event date, location and context.
- Cross-check significant, disputed or consequential claims against independent credible sources where available.
- Prefer primary records, direct statements, court documents, public datasets and original media when relevant.
- Attribute official statements as statements; do not imply they are independently established facts.
- Label allegations, opinion, analysis, projections and developing reports clearly.
- If evidence is insufficient, keep the item Not Verified or Disputed; do not publish it as confirmed.
- A failed, stale or unavailable feed must never be interpreted as evidence that no relevant event occurred.

## Required on-screen source panel
- GLOBAL FUNN • VERIFIED REPORT label only when automated evidence gates pass
- Headline and location
- Source references with visible links; seek independent corroboration for significant claims
- Publication timestamp and timezone; distinguish it from event time
- Status: Confirmed, Developing, Disputed or Not Verified
- AI illustration disclosure when applicable
- Correction/update history when applicable

## Images, video and sensitive material
- Never present AI-generated images as documentary evidence. Label them **AI ILLUSTRATION**.
- Check original date and location of reused footage; do not imply old footage is current.
- Do not publish graphic violence, gore, explicit sexual content, illegal instructions or glorification of harm.
- Respect privacy, dignity, consent and the safety of vulnerable people.
- Hold sensitive, disputed, legally consequential or insufficiently sourced items for human review; do not auto-publish them.

## Corrections and accountability
- Show corrections and update timestamps visibly.
- Preserve the original item, corrected wording, reason, responsible system/job identity and supporting references in an append-only audit trail.
- Do not silently erase material corrections.
- Record source evidence, script/media hashes, QC results, upload IDs and publication outcomes for every automated publication.

## Automated publication gate
- The channel owner has authorized routine, qualifying news updates to publish without per-video manual approval once the system is configured and tested.
- A publication worker may publish only when all required evidence, source, rights, safety, duplicate, media-QC and platform-policy checks pass.
- The worker must not infer approval from missing data, timeouts, partial checks or a failed dependency. Any uncertain result means hold, not publish.
- Sensitive, disputed, unsupported, privacy-invasive or high-consequence stories require human review and remain unpublished until resolved.
- The system must support an immediate owner-controlled emergency pause and maintain an auditable record of every publish/hold decision.
- This policy does not bypass YouTube API quota, OAuth consent, copyright law, platform rules or infrastructure requirements.
