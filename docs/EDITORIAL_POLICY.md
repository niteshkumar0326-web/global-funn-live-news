# GLOBAL FUNN LIVE NEWS — Editorial and Safety Policy

## Core standard
**No proof, no verified-news publication.** Every published report must expose its evidence references on screen and receive human editor approval.

## Evidence and attribution
- Never invent stories, quotes, sources, documents, URLs, dates, images or eyewitness accounts.
- A source URL is a pointer to inspect, not proof by itself.
- Verify the original source, author/publisher, publication date, event date, location and context.
- Cross-check significant, disputed or consequential claims against independent credible sources where available.
- Prefer primary records, direct statements, court documents, public datasets and original media when relevant.
- Attribute official statements as statements; do not imply they are independently established facts.
- Label allegations, opinion, analysis, projections and developing reports clearly.
- If evidence is insufficient, keep the item Not Verified or Disputed; do not publish it as confirmed.

## Required on-screen source panel
- GLOBAL FUNN • VERIFIED REPORT label only after editor approval
- Headline and location
- Source 1 and source 2 where available, with visible reference links
- Publication timestamp and timezone; distinguish it from event time
- Status: Confirmed, Developing, Disputed or Not Verified
- Editor approval state
- AI illustration disclosure when applicable
- Correction/update history when applicable

## Images, video and sensitive material
- Never present AI-generated images as documentary evidence. Label them **AI ILLUSTRATION**.
- Check original date and location of reused footage; do not imply old footage is current.
- Do not publish graphic violence, gore, explicit sexual content, illegal instructions or glorification of harm.
- Respect privacy, dignity, consent and the safety of vulnerable people.

## Corrections and accountability
- Show corrections and update timestamps visibly.
- Preserve the original item, corrected wording, reason, editor and supporting references in an audit trail.
- Do not silently erase material corrections.

## Publication gate
A human editor must approve the evidence, wording, visual material and final presentation before publication. No automated or background workflow may publish without explicit editor approval.
