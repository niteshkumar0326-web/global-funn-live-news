# GLOBAL FUNN Digital Business Revenue Map

## Strategic goal
Build a diversified digital business around original media, publishing, services and narrowly scoped software. The agent workflows support research, drafting, planning and quality control; they do not independently create customers, guarantee sales, render video or operate payments.

## Revenue portfolio

### Media and audience
- YouTube advertising and Premium revenue when each channel qualifies.
- Sponsorships, brand integrations and clearly disclosed affiliate commissions.
- Memberships, live events, paid community and fan support where eligible.
- Podcast sponsorships, newsletter sponsorships and paid newsletters.
- Licensing original footage, explainers, graphics and research packs.
- Syndication and regional-language adaptations with rights cleared.

### Client services
- Monthly Shorts/Reels packages; long-video repurposing.
- YouTube channel packaging, SEO metadata, analytics and editorial calendars.
- Hindi/English research briefs, scriptwriting, translation and localisation.
- Thumbnail/brand kits, product explainers and launch campaigns.
- White-label content production for marketing agencies.
- Business workflow setup: lead capture, order forms, reporting, CRM and customer follow-up drafts.
- Workshops and consulting for creators and small businesses.

### Publishing and knowledge products
- Kindle ebooks, print books, workbooks and journals.
- Practical guides, industry reports and paid research briefs.
- Downloadable templates, checklists, spreadsheets, prompt/workflow packs.
- Online courses, cohort workshops, recorded classes and paid communities.
- Licensing original training material to institutions or companies.

### Software and SaaS
- Subscription software for a clearly defined recurring workflow.
- One-time desktop/mobile tools, paid upgrades and maintenance.
- B2B setup, onboarding, customisation and support retainers.
- API access, usage-based plans, team seats and white-label licences.
- Micro-SaaS for creator research/briefs, local retailer orders, distributor reporting, inventory/collection reminders and regional content operations.
- Marketplace integrations and partner/referral revenue.

### Commerce and partnerships
- Affiliate comparison sites and buyer guides with disclosures.
- Digital storefronts and bundles.
- Print-on-demand merchandise using owned/licensed designs.
- Paid directories, job boards or niche databases only after demand validation.
- Referral partnerships with editing, hosting, design and business software providers.

## Product ladder
1. Free: useful videos, newsletter, checklist or sample.
2. Entry: ₹99–₹499 template, mini-guide or workbook (test prices, not market guarantees).
3. Core: ₹499–₹2,999 ebook/course/toolkit (test).
4. Service: ₹3,000–₹15,000 monthly content or operations package (scope-dependent test).
5. SaaS: ₹299–₹2,999 per month per account (validate willingness to pay and support economics).
6. B2B: custom onboarding, team licence or managed service, priced after discovery.

## Launch gates
- Evidence of a real buyer problem.
- Five to ten customer conversations or a small landing-page test.
- One manually delivered pilot before automation.
- Clear scope, pricing, refund, support and privacy terms.
- Unit economics include payment fees, taxes, hosting, APIs, editing, support and acquisition.
- Human approval for public claims, publishing, customer outreach, payment collection and production deployment.

## Recommended first experiments
1. Sell one monthly short-form content pilot to a real business.
2. Publish one useful ebook/workbook based on original expertise and validated demand.
3. Run a concierge pilot for a retailer/distributor daily order and field-report workflow.
4. Only then build the smallest SaaS feature that removes a repeated manual step.

## Compliance and trust
- Check current platform eligibility, fees, country/territory availability, tax and consumer rules.
- Obtain rights for music, footage, images, fonts, datasets and third-party material.
- Disclose affiliate links and sponsorships.
- Avoid fabricated reviews, mass-produced low-value content and unverified financial/medical/legal advice.
- Protect customer data, provide deletion/export paths and keep secrets out of Git.
