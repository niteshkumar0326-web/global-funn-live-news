# Books, SaaS and Digital Products — Build Roadmap

## A. Books and publishing

### First book concepts to validate
1. Practical AI tools for Indian small businesses (Hindi/English).
2. 30-day Hindi Shorts creator workbook.
3. Small distributor field-sales operating manual.
4. Retailer order, collection and sales-reporting playbook.
5. Beginner guide to regional YouTube research and SEO.
6. Plain-language guide to business communication and sales discipline.

Choose a subject where the author has original experience or can conduct credible research. Do not claim expertise not held. Start with a 15–25 page pilot guide or workbook; collect reader feedback before a full book.

### Publishing pipeline
Audience/problem → reader interviews → outline → source ledger → sample chapter → editorial review → manuscript → proofread → cover/layout → metadata/keywords → platform checks → publish → launch → reader feedback → revision.

Amazon KDP offers ebook and print-on-demand publishing; Indian-language ebook support includes Hindi, while Indian-language paperback publishing is currently unavailable per KDP's language guidance. Verify current territory, royalty and formatting rules at submission time.

## D. SaaS validation before coding
1. Interview at least five buyers in one niche.
2. Demonstrate a clickable mockup or manual concierge service.
3. Ask for a paid pilot or letter of intent; compliments are not validation.
4. Deliver manually for 2–4 weeks and record repeated actions.
5. Build only the most repeated and valuable workflow.
6. Test onboarding, permissions, backups, exports, deletion, security, support and billing.
7. Calculate gross margin and support cost before scaling.

## E. Software revenue models
- Monthly/annual subscriptions.
- Per-seat or per-location pricing.
- Usage-based API or credits.
- One-time licence with paid updates.
- Setup/onboarding fee plus monthly support.
- White-label or reseller licence.
- Custom implementation and training.
- Free tier with paid limits, only if support economics work.

## F. Implementation sequence
1. Validate RetailPulse manually because the workflow is concrete and measurable.
2. Build one small pilot with no real customer data until security and permissions are reviewed.
3. Validate CreatorDesk using the GLOBAL FUNN workflow internally.
4. Consider NewsProof Desk only after internal editorial workflow is stable.
5. Do not start three SaaS builds in parallel.

## G. India launch checklist
Check entity/tax obligations, GST applicability, invoicing, privacy notice, data retention, consumer/refund policy, payment gateway onboarding, subscription mandates, app-store terms, accessibility and support obligations. Seek qualified advice where required. Never store card or UPI credentials yourself.
